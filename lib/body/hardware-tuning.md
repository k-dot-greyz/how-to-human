# Hardware Tuning

Low-level firmware tweaks for physical presence. Optional. None of this is a moral upgrade.

## 1. `set_chassis_posture.sh` (structural integrity)

**Purpose:** Correct the default energy-saving slump, which reads as low confidence and slowly wrecks the chassis.

**Execution:**

1. `shoulders --set-state=back_and_down`
2. `spine --set-state=erect`
3. `chin --set-state=neutral`
4. Run whenever you pass a reflective surface (shop window, monitor). Aligned chassis improves coolant flow and the signal you broadcast.

## 2. `set_intake_manifold_config.sh` (mewing) — experimental

**Purpose:** Idle-state tongue posture some people use for jaw rest and nasal breathing. Treat as a background experiment, not a miracle firmware flash.

**Execution:**

1. Tongue to the roof of the mouth.
2. Light, consistent pressure.
3. Default idle, not a clenched performance.

## 3. `allocate_physical_memory.sh` (take up space)

**Purpose:** Counter the habit of minimizing your footprint until the process is starved of RAM.

**Execution:**

1. Seated: expand peripherals to a comfortable, non-compacted state. Use the armrests.
2. Standing: wider stance than the default apology.
3. Not aggression. Just the allocation your process actually needs.
